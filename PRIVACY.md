# Privacy Policy

Hovercraft does not collect, store, sell or send any of your data.

- Everything runs locally in your browser on x.com and twitter.com.
- The extension reads profile data that X has already loaded on the page, only to draw the hover card.
- It makes no network requests of its own, except one: when you click **Follow** or **Following** in the card, it sends that follow/unfollow request to X using your existing X session, the same as clicking X's own button.
- It has no analytics, no tracking and no remote servers.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/nchudleigh/hovercraft/issues
