## Store listing

**Name:** Hovercraft

**Summary (≤132 chars):** Instant profile hover cards on X. No wait, no fade, and the card glides between profiles as you move.

**Category:** Social & Communication

**Description:**

Hovercraft replaces X's profile hover card with one that keeps up with you.

• Shows the moment you hover a profile, with no half-second wait and no fade
• Hides the moment you move away, unless you're moving into the card
• Glides from profile to profile instead of closing and reopening, so you can sweep across a row of avatars
• Uses data X already loaded, so there's no network wait

The card shows the avatar, a Follow button, name and verified badge, "Follows you", bio, follower counts and "Followed by…". It matches X's Light, Dim and Lights out themes.

Open source: https://github.com/nchudleigh/hovercraft

**Screenshots:** 1280×800 or 640×400, at least one. Take them on X with the card open.

## Privacy practices

**Single purpose:** Replace the profile hover card on X with a faster one.

**Host permission justification (x.com, twitter.com):** The extension only runs on X. It needs page access there to detect profile hovers, read the profile data already on the page, and draw its card.

**Remote code:** No, I am not using remote code.

**Data usage:** Collects none of the listed data types. Tick all three certifications (no selling, no unrelated use, no creditworthiness use).
